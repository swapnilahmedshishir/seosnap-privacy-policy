Privacy Policy

SEOSnap — AI Image SEO & Alt Text

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SEOSnap (the “App”), provided by Xetriva (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use the App. SEOSnap helps you analyze images and create SEO content such as alt text, titles, descriptions, keywords, and structured data.

At a glance: Image labeling, text recognition, color extraction, and several other features are processed on your device. Online AI generation, advertising, and subscription features require communication with their respective service providers. Please review the details below before using those features.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, the App may process the following information:

  • Images you choose or capture: Photos selected from your gallery or captured with the camera for image analysis and SEO generation.
  • Image-derived information: Object labels, text recognized from an image (OCR), dominant colors, image dimensions, file size, format, and available image metadata.
  • SEO inputs and results: Your optional focus keyword, selected language and writing tone, generated SEO fields, edits, and related SEO scores or checklist results.
  • Image metadata: The App may read available EXIF information, which can include the capture date, camera model, orientation, and GPS coordinates if those details are embedded in the image.
  • App preferences and local records: Theme, language and tone preferences, credit or cached subscription status, and generation history stored by the App.
  • Purchase information: Subscription purchase and entitlement status handled through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details.
  • Advertising and technical information: Information that may be processed by the advertising or app-security services described below, such as ad interactions, device/app identifiers, diagnostic data, and consent choices, subject to those providers’ policies and settings.

2. How Images Are Analyzed

SEOSnap uses on-device technologies, including Google ML Kit, to perform image labeling and text recognition. Color extraction and image-property inspection are also performed locally. These on-device analysis features can work without an internet connection.

Image history and related records are stored in the App’s local Room database on your device. App preferences and certain cached values are stored locally using Android DataStore. The App does not require you to create an account to use its core local features.

When you use an online AI feature, the App may send the information needed to fulfill your request—such as image-analysis results, recognized text, your optional keyword, selected tone and language, and refinement instructions—to the relevant AI service. The exact information transmitted can depend on the feature and request. Do not submit confidential, personal, or sensitive images or text unless you are comfortable with the processing described in this policy.

3. AI Services and Third-Party Providers

Online AI generation and refinement may use the following services:

  • Google Firebase AI Logic / Gemini: Used for online AI-powered SEO generation and refinement.
  • OpenRouter: May be used as a fallback service to access available AI models, which can include models from third-party providers.
  • Google ML Kit: Used for on-device image labeling and text recognition.

When a request is routed through an AI provider, the information included in that request is processed under that provider’s terms and privacy policy. Providers may process information in locations outside your country. We do not control the independent privacy practices, retention rules, or infrastructure of third-party AI providers. Please consult their current documentation before using online AI features.

4. Advertising

The free version of SEOSnap may display banner, interstitial, or rewarded advertisements through Google Mobile Ads. Advertising providers may collect or receive device identifiers, approximate device or network information, ad interaction data, and other information as described in their own policies. The availability and personalization of ads may depend on your region, consent choices, device settings, and applicable law.

Rewarded ads are optional and may provide additional in-app credits. Premium subscribers are intended to receive an ad-free experience.

For more information, review Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising information.

5. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

SEOSnap offers optional premium subscriptions through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes payments and provides purchase or subscription status needed to activate, restore, or verify premium access. Payment processing is governed by Google Play’s terms and privacy practices. We do not collect your full card number or banking credentials through the App.

You can manage or cancel a subscription through your Google Play account settings. Uninstalling the App does not automatically cancel a subscription.

6. How We Use Information

Information processed by the App and its service providers is used, as applicable, to:

  • Analyze selected images and generate, refine, display, and export SEO content.
  • Save and retrieve your local generation history and preferences.
  • Calculate SEO scores and provide optimization suggestions.
  • Provide credits, process subscriptions, and restore premium entitlements.
  • Display and measure advertisements in the free version.
  • Protect the services, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and maintain app functionality.

7. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Generation history, preferences, and related local records are stored on your device. You can delete individual history records or clear your history using the controls available in the App. You can also remove locally stored App data by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling it, subject to Android’s device and backup settings.

Information sent to third-party AI, advertising, app-security, or billing providers may be retained by those providers according to their own policies. Deleting local App data does not necessarily delete information already processed by a third-party provider.

SEOSnap’s described core workflow does not depend on a Xetriva-operated user account or cloud history service. If a future version adds account-based or cloud features, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant processing.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate features you choose, including AI generation, advertising, app integrity/security, and Google Play purchases. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and the security of the App.
